Key concepts

How the two experiments fit together.

Dictionary attack

A guess list puts common passwords first. Time to reach a guess depends on its position and the guessing rate.

Unique salts

A random salt per password forces attackers to calculate guesses separately for each differently salted record.

Cost per guess

Repeated hashing work raises the cost of every guess. Long, randomly generated passwords also make likely guesses less effective.
